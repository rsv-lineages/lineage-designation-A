# 2025-08-23

Correct mutation numbering of the M2-2 protein: In an early annotation used in the Nextstrain builds, the M2-2 gene was annotated incorrectly. The start of the translated region was 6 bases too early. The correct annotation (according to [UniProt](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P88812/entry)) has the protein start at the methionine 6 bases later. All clade definitions using substitutions in M2-2 have been revised to reflect that change. This affects A.D.3.11, A.D.3.3, A.D.3.5, A.D.3.6, A.D.5.2, and A.D.5.3.
